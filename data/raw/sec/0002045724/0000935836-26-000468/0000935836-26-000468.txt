Under the Registration Rights Agreement, the Issuer was required to file the Registration Statement with the Commission no later than the 45th calendar day following the date of the Registration Rights Agreement. The Issuer was required to use its reasonable best efforts to cause the Registration Statement to be declared effective by the Commission no later than the 60th calendar day following the date of the Registration Rights Agreement (or the 90th calendar day in the event of a "full review" by the Commission). The Registration Statement was required to be on Form S-3 (or, if the Issuer is not then eligible to use Form S-3, on another appropriate form).

The maximum aggregate liquidated damages payable to the Fund under the Registration Rights Agreement is 5.0% of the aggregate subscription amount paid by the Fund pursuant to the Purchase Agreement.
